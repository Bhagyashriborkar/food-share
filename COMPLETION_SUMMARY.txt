## 📊 DATABASE CHANGES

### New Table: `HistoryLog`
Stores permanent records of all completed donations:
```
- claim_id (reference to original)
- food_title (what was donated)
- donor_id & ngo_id (who was involved)
- quantity_served & people_served
- pickup_time & completion_time
- archived_at (when archived)
```

### Updated: `Claim` Table
```
Added fields:
- otp_code (6-digit string)
- otp_verified (boolean)
- people_served (integer)
Updated status field to include 'completed'
```
